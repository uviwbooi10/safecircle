SafeCircle

You should not have to explain yourself to get help.

SafeCircle gives you a fast way to reach the people you trust, plain-language information about what abuse actually looks like, and a place to say what happened — anonymously, if that is easier.

Where responsibility sits. Nothing on this site is here because anyone being hurt has done something wrong. Abuse is a choice made by the person doing it. SafeCircle exists to make getting help faster — not to put the job of stopping it on you.

Panic alert

Press the button three times

Three presses starts a five-second countdown, then sends your circle your location and a short message. You can cancel during the countdown. Nothing is typed, so it works when you cannot talk.

Presses reset after two seconds of no activity.

This is a prototype, so no message actually leaves your device. On the real site this would send an SMS and a push notification to everyone in your circle.

My circle

The people who get alerted

Keep it small — three or four people who would actually pick up. They can see when you last checked in, and you can see theirs.

Resources

Real numbers, free to call

These are national South African services. All of them are free and most run twenty-four hours.

If you are in immediate danger, call 10111 first. Everything else on this page can wait until you are somewhere safe.

Awareness

What it looks like before it looks like abuse

Tick anything that feels familiar — about your own relationship, or someone else's. Nothing is saved anywhere but this device, and you can clear it at any time.

Nothing ticked yet. Read through the list at your own pace.

Share your story

Say it in your own words

You do not need a name, a full story, or the right words. Write as little as you want. If writing into the open feels like too much, talk to a listener first instead.

Posted as Anonymous. Nothing identifying is attached.

From other people

This listener is a prototype demonstration, not a counsellor and not a real person. It cannot help in an emergency. For real support, the numbers under Resources are free and staffed by trained people.
